About UTIC

United Transformative Industries Co. Ltd. (UTIC) is a family-owned Saudi company based in Jeddah. Our team of architects, civil engineers and project professionals delivers engineering and design work developed in line with Saudi building codes.

The company has been led by its Chairman, Dr. Majed K. A., since 2018.

Services

Architectural design

Residential, commercial, administrative, educational, healthcare and hospitality buildings, designed to Saudi building codes.

Infrastructure engineering

Civil engineering consulting for infrastructure works.

Soil and ground studies

Geotechnical assessment to inform safe, sound design.

Quality control

Checks that work meets the required standards and regulations.

Project management

Planning, scheduling and coordination from concept to completion.

Supporting Saudi Vision 2030

The Kingdom is transforming how it builds, lives and works. As a Saudi engineering company in Jeddah, we aim to contribute disciplined engineering and thoughtful design to that transformation, and to develop local talent along the way.

Values and safety

  • Integrity – straightforward with clients, partners and each other.
